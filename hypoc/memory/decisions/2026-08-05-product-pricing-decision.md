# Product Pricing Decision

## Decision

The team decided to implement usage-based pricing with a free tier of one thousand tokens per month.

## Rationale

Cost tracks value delivered, and a free tier lowers onboarding friction.
## Alternatives considered

- Flat monthly subscription
- Per-seat pricing
